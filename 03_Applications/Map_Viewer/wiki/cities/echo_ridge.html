Echo Ridge is a village located in NorKunta, Antarmund. It is situated at an altitude of 3475m and is characterized by a Mountain Mining climate.

Description

Small mining outpost extracting rare earth metals from the ridge.

Visual Profile

"A precarious mining outpost clinging to a jagged, wind-swept mountain ridge. Scaffolding and drilling rigs hang over the edge. Large rock-crushers pulverize stone, creating dust clouds that mix with the snow. Industrial floodlights cut through the dust. The architecture is temporary, industrial, and bolted directly into the rock."
