Skýjakot is a metropolis located in Gryning, Nordica. It is situated at an altitude of 1213m and is characterized by a Boreal or Alpine Forest climate.

Description

The 'Cloud House' built atop the Taiga canopy.

History & Lore

Skýjakot, meaning "Cloud House," defies the gloom of Gryning's dense forests by being built atop the canopy layer, showcasing sleek glass and wood architecture that integrates with massive vertical wind-shafts. The metropolis serves as a beacon of innovation and a testament to Nordican ingenuity, harnessing the power of the winds while offering its inhabitants a brighter, more connected existence above the shadowy forest floor.
