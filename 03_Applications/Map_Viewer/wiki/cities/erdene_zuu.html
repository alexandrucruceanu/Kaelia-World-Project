Erdene Zuu is a special located in Oighear, Nordica. It is situated at an altitude of 492m and is characterized by a Cold Desert climate.

Description

Ancient temple complex with digital vaults.

History & Lore

Erdene Zuu stands as an ancient temple complex nestled within the Oighear's frozen expanse, a testament to Nordica's rich history. Now, alongside its spiritual significance, it hosts digital archival vaults, preserving knowledge within the heart of the Frost-Lands as a beacon of wisdom amidst the icy plains.
