Nechars is a special located in The Dark-Water States, The Mirelands. It is situated at an altitude of 811m and is characterized by a Permafrost Vaults climate.

Description

Guardians of the Permafrost Vaults.

History & Lore

Northernmost frontier; guardians of the Permafrost Vaults.

Visual Profile

"The northernmost frontier. A high-tech outpost built on deep permafrost. Architecture features heavy insulation and heat-sinking pylons. Permafrost seed vaults and digital archival archives deep underground. Steam discharging into the frozen air."
