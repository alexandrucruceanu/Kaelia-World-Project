Niruz is a settlement located in The Dark-Water States, The Mirelands. It is situated at an altitude of 33m and is characterized by a Agri-Tech climate.

Description

Powerhouse for flood-resistant crops.

History & Lore

Agri-tech powerhouse specializing in flood-resistant smart-crops.
