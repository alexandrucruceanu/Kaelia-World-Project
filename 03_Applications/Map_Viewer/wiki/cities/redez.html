Redez is a metropolis located in The Dark-Water States, The Mirelands. It is situated at an altitude of 10m and is characterized by a Wetland Rim climate.

Description

Industrial wetland rim.

History & Lore

Industrial wetland rim; specialized in amphibious logistics.

Visual Profile

"A city on the edge of the world-ocean built for Muskeg terrain. Buildings are built on massive pontoons. Amphibious trucks with oversized tracks moving between floating warehouses over ground too soft for wheels."
