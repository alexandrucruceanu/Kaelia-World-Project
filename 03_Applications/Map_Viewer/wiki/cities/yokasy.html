Yokasy is a metropolis located in The Golden Isles, Kasy Federation. It is situated at an altitude of 5m and is characterized by a Chaparral climate.

Description

Subterranean metropolis; MAGLEV terminal.

History & Lore

Subterranean metropolis; architecture uses light-shafts and natural cooling. The world's primary MAGLEV terminal.

Visual Profile

"A cross-section of a subterranean metropolis in a rolling Chaparral scrubland. Massive geometric light-wells carved through red sandstone. Surface area dominated by olive and cork agriculture. MAGLEV trains gliding through glass tubes beneath the fire-resistant city shell."
